Chemistry Laboratory

Exploring the principles of matter, composition, physical and chemical reactions and energy.

History of the Chemistry Laboratory

Early chemistry practicals focused on foundational wet chemistry using glass burettes, pipettes, spirit lamps, Bunsen burners, and basic volumetric or qualitative analysis reagents The re-innovation of the chemistry lab emphasizes safety, environmental sustainability, and precision.

Purpose of the Laboratory

The laboratory provides students with practical opportunities to understand engage in experiments that mirror modern industrial and pharmaceutical standards, moving beyond basic test-tube reactions into sophisticated molecular analysis.

Laboratory Equipments

Some examples of Chemistry equipments include:

  • Burretes
  • pipettes
  • Bunsen burners
  • Beaker
  • Conical flasks
  • Test tubes , etc.
