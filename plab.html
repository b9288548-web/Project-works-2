Physics Laboratory

Exploring the principles of matter, energy, motion and physical phenomena.

History of the Physics Laboratory

In its early decades, the physics lab relied heavily on classic mechanical apparatuses—such as wooden inclined planes, tuning forks, analog galvanometers, pendulum setups, and heavy brass weights—to demonstrate classical mechanics, optics, and thermodynamics. Today, the lab has integrated digital transformation. Traditional meters have been supplemented with computer-based data loggers, motion sensors, digital oscilloscopes, and real-time data acquisition systems.

Purpose of the Laboratory

The laboratory provides students with practical opportunities to understand physics through experiments and scientific investigations.

Laboratory Equipment
