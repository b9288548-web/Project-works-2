Biology Laboratory

Exploring structure,behaviour and relationships between living organisms.

History of the Physics Laboratory

The biology lab centeres on microscopy, using basic compound light microscopes, anatomical charts, and manual dissection kits for studying plant and animal specimens Re-innovation efforts have also introduced modern genetic and ecological kits, empowering students to explore biotechnology, cellular biology, and environmental science with greater depth of knowledge.

Purpose of the Laboratory

The laboratory ensures that its students do not just learn science from textbooks but experience it firsthand through modern, biological-standard tools.

Laboratory Equipments.

  • Anatomical charts.
  • Petri dish.
  • Microscopes, etc.
