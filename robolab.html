STEM Laboratory

The STEM lab serves as a breeding ground for success in global competitions. The 2024 victory in ROBOFEST WORLD CHAMPIONSHIPS held in the United States signifies Prempeh College's rich historical track record in global robotics (following prior stellar runs in 2019,2020, and 2023), solidifying its reputation as an elite champion globally.

Purpose of the Laboratory

The laboratory provides students with skills in engineering and equip them towards world-class competitions.
