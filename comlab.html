Computer Science Laboratory

Exploring technological skills of students and ensuring specialized software development environment for students.

History of the Computer Science Laboratory

The lab in its nascent stages involved basic standalone desktop computers with limited processing power, primarily used for introductory typing, basic programming languages (like BASIC), and theoretical computer studies but has been re-innovated with powerful desktop computers.

Purpose of the Laboratory

It serves as the digital foundation where students transition from simple coding to advanced software engineering, machine learning, and robotics integration.

Laboratory Equipments

Some examples devices in the lab include:

  • Desktop Computers
  • System units
  • Projectors, etc.
